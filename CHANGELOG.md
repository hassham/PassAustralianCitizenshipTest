# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-08-09)

Initial release.

- 421 practice questions covering all four official Australian Citizenship
  Test categories: Australia and its people, Australia's democratic beliefs,
  government and the law, and Australian values.
- Practice by category or across all categories, with instant feedback,
  explanations, and source references for every answer.
- Mock exams that mirror the real test format, including the requirement to
  answer every Australian values question correctly to pass.
- Progress tracking, exam history and review, and starred questions for
  focused revision.
- Fully offline after install. No account required, and no data is
  collected, transmitted, or sold.
- Free, with no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.
