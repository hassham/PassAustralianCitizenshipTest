# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 23 July 2026

Pass Australian Citizenship Test is an offline-first study application.

## Data collection

The application does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

## Data stored on the device

Practice progress, starred questions, mock-exam attempts, and application preferences are stored locally on the user's device. Users can reset progress or clear starred questions from Settings.

## Network access

Core study features work offline. The application may open an external browser when the user chooses a privacy, licence, source, or support link. Those external services have their own privacy practices.

## Purchases

If in-app purchases are introduced, payment processing will be handled by Google Play or Apple. This policy will be updated before purchase features are released.

## Contact

Questions or issues can be submitted through the project's public GitHub support page:

https://github.com/hassham/PassAustralianCitizenshipTest/issues

## Changes

This policy may be updated when application features or store requirements change.
